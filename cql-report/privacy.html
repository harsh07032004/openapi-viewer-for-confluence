Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-19. Applies to CQL Report for Confluence by TitanLabs.

The app stores no data. Each report's query and display settings are saved by Confluence as part of the page, like any other macro. Searches run in the viewer's browser through Confluence's own API as that person. The app makes no external network calls, sends nothing to us or third parties, and uses no analytics, tracking or cookies. Contact: https://github.com/harsh07032004/openapi-viewer-for-confluence/issues.
