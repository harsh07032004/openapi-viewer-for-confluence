Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-19. Applies to OpenAPI & Swagger Viewer for Confluence ("the app") by TitanLabs ("we").

What the app stores

  • The OpenAPI or Swagger text you paste or upload, its title and your display options. The app may also keep up to two previous saved versions of the text so readers can see what changed; older versions are deleted automatically when you save.
  • The ID of the Confluence page or blog post that holds the macro, used to enforce page permissions.

The app does not store names, email addresses, Atlassian account IDs or any other personal data.

Where it is stored

All data is stored in Atlassian Forge storage for your Atlassian site. The app runs on Atlassian infrastructure, declares no external network access, and does not send your data to us or to any third party. We do not use analytics, tracking or cookies.

Access

The app contains no feature that sends your stored data to us. Atlassian processes the data as the hosting provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

Logs

When an error occurs, the Forge platform records technical error details (not spec contents) so we can fix problems.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in your site's Forge storage while the app is installed. Removing the macro from a page stops the spec from being shown, but the stored copy remains until the app is uninstalled. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes its stored data under the Forge data retention rules. We cannot read or delete your site's stored data ourselves.
